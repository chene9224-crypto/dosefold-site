Privacy, without fine print

Your journal belongs to you.

Dosefold is built local-first. Your medication, meals, water, weight and goals stay on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, and no advertising profile.

  • ✓Local by default. Your journal is stored on this device, not in a social or advertising cloud.
  • ✓Optional is optional. FoodScan is used only when you choose it. Photos and text are sent to the scan service only for the request you initiate.
  • ✓No surprises. We do not sell your personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
